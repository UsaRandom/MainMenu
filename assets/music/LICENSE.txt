  UNRECORDED: the collection these came from is not named anywhere in this repository

  fixing rather than leaving. If you know where these came from, put it here.

These are notes, not recordings. The menu synthesises them at play time with midi64
(src/libs/midi64, MIT), which is why 28 songs cost 296 KB. The instrument patches are
midi64's, under its own licence, not part of this dedication.
