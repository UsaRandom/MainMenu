# Back out of a game sheet and land where you left, not at the top of the tab.
#
# The grid used to reset the cursor and the scroll in grid_enter(), which runs on every entry and
# not only the first. So opening the fortieth title and pressing B put you back among the first
# four with forty presses between you and where you had been -- on the single journey people make
# most often in a launcher.
#
# The frames either side of the sheet are the whole test: 1 and 3 must show the same selected
# tile at the same scroll offset, and 3 must not be the top of the library. A hash comparison
# alone would not say that, because the two frames legitimately differ -- the sheet leaves the
# arrival tween restarted -- so this one is read, not just diffed.

wait 120

# Onto N64, because the fixture opens on Recent and Recent holds two titles -- a tab that does
# not scroll cannot show a scroll being lost.
press r x2
wait 20

press down x3       # three rows down, well past anything visible at rest
press right x2
wait 30
fbdump              # 1: somewhere in the middle of the library

press a
wait 40
fbdump              # 2: the sheet for that title

press b
wait 40
fbdump              # 3: MUST match frame 1's selection and scroll, not the top of the tab

exit
