# Scroll far enough, and back, that the thumbnail working set is evicted and refilled twice.
#
# The point is to keep the decoder permanently behind. browse-roms lands, steps around and
# settles, so most of its frames are drawn against art that is already resident and it reports
# a frame rate the product does not actually have while you are moving. Here the selection never
# stops long enough for the cache to catch up, which is the state the frame budget has to
# survive and the only state in which the decode budget means anything.
#
# Long by design: the interesting numbers are the steady-state ones, and a run short enough to
# finish before the first eviction measures the startup transient instead.

wait 60             # library scan, first screen of art

press down x10      # straight down past the bottom of the resident set
wait 40
fbdump              # 1: deep in the library, mid-refill

press down x10      # keep going, never letting the cache land
wait 40
fbdump              # 2: deeper still

press up x18        # all the way back, evicting everything just decoded
wait 40
fbdump              # 3: returned to the top, refilled from scratch

press down x6       # settle somewhere with art resident
wait 90             # long enough for the decoder to actually finish
fbdump              # 4: at rest, everything on screen decoded

exit
