# Jump straight to the end of the N64 tab, where the real 1000 px scans and the portrait
# Japanese cards live, and sit there long enough for them to decode.
#
# This is the case that catches priority inversion: with the decoder walking the library from
# index 0, nothing here would ever load.

wait 30
press down x9       # to the bottom of the library
wait 900            # long enough to measure time-to-fill, not just progress
fbdump              # 1: end of library, real scans

press up x4
wait 400
fbdump              # 2: mid-library

exit
